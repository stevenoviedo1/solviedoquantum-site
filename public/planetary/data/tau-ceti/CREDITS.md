# Tau Ceti — uncertainty & sources

Tau Ceti (G8V, ~11.9 ly) has a well-established debris disk. Planetary **candidates** from radial-velocity work have been proposed and debated.

Walkable Worlds shows:

- Schematic orbital layout (not dynamical integration)
- Explicit **candidate** / **speculative** labels
- Optional speculative surface walks for HZ candidates e/f

Sources for further reading:

- https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/
- https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/tau-ceti-e/
- https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/tau-ceti-f/

Do not treat candidate planets as confirmed Earth-like worlds.
